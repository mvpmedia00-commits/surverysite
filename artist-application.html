Who This Is For

Artist Roles

This application is for visual artists of all ages: painters, drawing artists, illustrators, digital artists, sketch artists, mural artists, prop painters, makeup artists, and creative collaborators who want to help develop or execute visual concepts.

You do not need body-paint experience to apply. Body paint is one possible project type, but drawing, painting, illustration, design, murals, props, backdrops, and digital art are welcome too.

Drawing & PaintingShare your style, technique, subject matter, and examples of finished or in-progress work.
Concept DevelopmentCreate sketches, mood boards, references, character ideas, styling concepts, or visual plans.
Creative ProductionAssist with murals, props, painted backdrops, wearable art, set pieces, or collaborative projects.
