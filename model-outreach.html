Two Ways to Work With MVP Media

Not every body paint model serves the same purpose, and each session is built around a planned studio goal.

MVP Media works with models in two different capacities: recurring models who serve as regular canvases while I develop and practice body-paint techniques, and professional models selected for completed creative productions.

The model role is to bring reliability, patience, presence, and the ability to work within the direction provided. Applicants can be considered for one or both tracks, but final placement is based on fit for the concept and production needs.

Direction, Limits, Expectations

Figure Modeling & Body Paint

Both regular canvas sessions and professional productions may involve artistic figure modeling.

Some concepts are built for figure work, artistic glamour, or classic studio presentation. That may require the model to pose unclothed while cosmetic body paint is applied directly to the skin.

The concept, required coverage, areas being painted, posing expectations, photography, compensation, and non-negotiable limits are discussed before accepting a session.

Direction: MVP Media sets the visual goal, posing style, and final image direction before the shoot.

Limits: Body paint coverage, figure modeling comfort, and non-negotiable boundaries are discussed before booking.

Expectations: Models should arrive ready to follow the agreed concept, timing, stillness, and camera direction.

Nothing is left to chance on shoot day. MVP Media sets the direction, and every look, pose, and expectation is locked in before the session begins. If a required element falls outside those pre-agreed limits, the project simply isn’t the right fit.

What Happens During a Body Paint Session?

01 — Pre-Shoot Discussion

Review the concept, compensation, expected session length, required look, wardrobe or figure-modeling requirements, body paint coverage, photography plans, limits, and image usage.

02 — Preparation

Prepare the studio, paints, brushes, lighting, and model for the body painting process.

03 — Body Paint Application

The model serves as the live canvas while professional cosmetic-grade body paint is applied. Painting may require standing, sitting, changing positions, and remaining relatively still for periods of time. Breaks are provided as needed.

04 — Final Artwork

The completed design is reviewed and final details or touch-ups are added.

05 — Photography

The completed artwork may be photographed using glamour, editorial, fine-art, portrait, or classic figure posing.

06 — Cleanup

The session concludes and the model can remove the body paint.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need modeling experience?

No previous modeling experience is required for the regular canvas model opportunity. Previous modeling experience is preferred for professional body paint productions.

Do I need body paint experience?

No.

Is this figure modeling?

Some projects involve artistic figure modeling for glamour, body-paint, or classic visual concepts. The specific requirements are discussed before booking.

Will body paint be applied directly to my skin?

Yes. Body paint projects involve cosmetic body paint being applied directly to the model’s skin.

How long does body painting take?

It depends on the complexity of the design. Applicants should expect that detailed body painting can require extended periods of standing or posing.

Can I take breaks?

Yes. Breaks can be incorporated during longer sessions.

Are limits discussed before booking?

Yes. Limits, body paint coverage, posing requirements, and comfort levels are discussed before booking. MVP Media selects models whose limits fit the concept; the concept is not redesigned on shoot day.

Will I be photographed?

Most body paint projects are created for photography. The photography is directed around MVP Media's glamour, fine-art, and editorial point of view, and intended image usage is discussed before booking.

Do I need to send nude photographs to apply?

No. Applicants are not required to submit nude photographs as part of the initial application process. A normal modeling portfolio, social media portfolio, headshot, or recent clothed photographs can be used for initial review.

Is there an age requirement?

Yes. Applicants must be at least 18 years old. Valid identification will be verified before booking.
